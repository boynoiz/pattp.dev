Hello! I'm Patt, a DevSecOps/SRE Engineer and programmer at heart, based in Bangkok, Thailand. Welcome to my corner of the internet, where I share my projects, tutorials, travel adventures, and notes from my journey.

**What I'm Doing Now:**

* Working as a Senior DevSecOps Engineer at [Conicle](https://www.conicle.com/).
* Go is my primary language these days — I've written a bunch of CLI tools and automation with it (an SSH tunnel manager, a code generator, and more).
* Diving deeper into Rust — my biggest project so far is a spec-driven, scenario-based load-testing tool built on Goose.
* Occasionally learning Zig for systems-level and performance-critical work.
* Building a home lab Kubernetes cluster on four Raspberry Pis, with NAS for persistent storage.
* Tinkering with IoT — Arduino, ESP32, and Seeed Studio "Grove" sensors — for a smart farm project.

**Facts About Me:**

* I started my tech journey at 16, learning HTML in internet cafes and creating websites with GeoCities (earning money from ad banners!).
* I've worked at an internet cafe (fixing computers, building networks, and learning LAMP stack web development).
* My entrepreneurial ventures include a computer shop and a printing business.
* My career path has evolved: Graphic Designer → Web Developer → DevOps Engineer / Site Reliability Engineer. The title changed over the years; the love of building never did.
* I'm an outdoor person at heart. Big hikes are rarer these days, but I still love car camping — finding a quiet spot with friends, cooking a good meal, and reflecting on life. I still dream of climbing Mount Mokoju!
* Running and badminton keep me moving.
* I've played guitar since I was 14 — still not that skilled, but I love it. My current one is a PRS SE Paul's Guitar (2021).
* Lifelong anime and manga fan, ever since watching Dragon Ball on Thai Channel 9 as a kid. These days I lean toward isekai, mystery, and the occasional story that wrecks me emotionally.
* Learning Japanese as my third language — 日本語は難しいですが、勉強は大好きです！
